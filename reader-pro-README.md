**Reel Counting System** — counts your reels while you sleep.

Reader Pro reads Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels from your own Google Sheets and writes back the exact counts: **Views, Likes, Comments, Shares** — plus author followers. No typing numbers by hand, no screenshots, no guesswork.
