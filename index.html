Reader Pro

Reel Counting System

Reader Pro reads Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels from your own Google Sheets and writes back the exact counts. No typing numbers by hand, no screenshots, no guesswork.

⬇ Download for Android
Version 1.6.4 · Free 7-day trial starts the moment you sign in

What it counts

Views · Likes · Comments · Shares — exact numbers, plus author followers.
Results come back sheet-ready, row by row, in the same order as your sheet.

How it works

  1. Install and sign in — open the app, type your email, a code arrives in your inbox. Type it and you are in.
  2. Paste your sheet link — give it the Google Sheet that holds your reel links, pick the rows.
  3. Press start — the app opens each reel, reads the counts on screen, and saves your results file.

First install? Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser — tap Allow, then open the downloaded file. Inside the app, turn on its Accessibility switch once (Settings → Installed apps → Reader Pro) so it can read reels for you.
