Three steps. Then put the phone down.

01

Install and sign in. Open the app, type your email, and a code arrives in your inbox. Type the code — you are in, and your trial starts.

02

One switch. Turn on the app's Accessibility switch once (Settings → Installed apps → Reader Pro) so it can read reels for you.

03

Paste your sheet and press start. Give it the Google Sheet that holds your links, pick your rows, and it counts — saving a results file in the same order as your sheet.

One honest thing: Reader Pro is not on the Play Store, because apps that read other apps through Accessibility are not allowed there. That is why you download it from this page — Android will ask you to allow the install, and that is normal for apps installed this way.

Questions, answered straight.

Is the trial really free?

Yes — 7 full days from the moment you sign in. No card, no payment details asked.

What numbers do I get?

Views, Likes, Comments and Shares for every reel — exact numbers with commas, in the same row order as your sheet, ready to paste back.

What do I need before starting?

An Android phone, Instagram / YouTube / Facebook signed in, and a Google Sheet of reel links shared as “Anyone with the link”.

What happens when my time ends?

The app stops at the gate and tells you. Everything you already counted stays on your phone — it is never taken away.
